Project: AegisAI – Autonomous Decision Guardrail Agent

==================================================
1. PROJECT SUMMARY
==================================================
AegisAI is an AI-powered decision evaluation system that acts as a guardrail
for organizations by analyzing proposed decisions and predicting risks before
those decisions are executed.

The system simulates the role of a Risk Officer, CTO, or Governance Committee.

==================================================
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
==================================================
Organizations often make high-impact decisions without fully understanding
second-order risks, operational fragility, or downstream consequences.

AegisAI addresses this gap by providing structured, forward-looking risk
analysis powered by an LLM.

==================================================
3. CORE CONCEPT
==================================================
Unlike traditional monitoring tools that report past events, AegisAI evaluates
future risk by reasoning over context, constraints, and trade-offs.

Key idea:
Present → Possible Futures → Risk & Guardrails

==================================================
4. COMPLETE TECHNOLOGY STACK
==================================================

Backend:
- Python 3
- FastAPI
- Uvicorn
- Requests

AI / Reasoning Layer:
- Ollama (local LLM runtime)
- LLaMA 3
- Prompt-based reasoning agent

Frontend:
- HTML5
- CSS3 (control-room style UI)
- Vanilla JavaScript

Architecture:
- Agent-based design
- Stateless reasoning with short-term memory
- JSON-validated AI outputs
- Human-in-the-loop decision workflow

==================================================
5. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

Decision Proposal
   ↓
Risk Reasoning Agent (LLM)
   ↓
Risk Scoring & Classification
   ↓
Guardrails & Monitoring Signals
   ↓
Executive-Ready Output
   ↓
Web Control Panel

==================================================
6. KEY FEATURES
==================================================
- Risk level classification (LOW → CRITICAL)
- Confidence-weighted risk scoring
- Primary and second-order risk detection
- Guardrail recommendations
- Monitoring signals
- 30-day scenario outlook
- Decision recommendation (Approve / Revise / Block)

==================================================
7. WHY THIS PROJECT IS UNIQUE
==================================================
- Focuses on decision safety, not chat
- Forward-looking risk prediction
- Enterprise governance mindset
- Structured, executive-ready output
- Local LLM for privacy and control

==================================================
8. INTERVIEW QUESTIONS & ANSWERS
==================================================

Q: What does AegisAI do?
A: It evaluates proposed decisions and predicts operational, financial, and
reputational risks before execution.

Q: How is this different from chatbots?
A: Chatbots answer questions. AegisAI reasons about decisions and produces
governance-grade risk assessments.

Q: Why use an LLM?
A: LLMs enable contextual reasoning across complex trade-offs without relying
on static rules.

Q: Is this production-ready?
A: The architecture is production-ready; enterprise features like persistence,
authentication, and integrations would be added for deployment.

Q: What kind of decisions can it analyze?
A: Technical, operational, compliance, financial, and organizational decisions.
